MFE shear flow: bifurcations and Lagrangian descriptors

This site uses the nine-mode Galerkin model of Moehlis, Faisst and Eckhardt (MFE) for sinusoidal shear flow between free-slip walls as a worked example for LagrangianDescriptors.jl, together with a bifurcation analysis with BifurcationKit.jl.

Pages

  • Model: the equations, two independent implementations (explicit transcription and Galerkin projection) and their agreement, laminar stability.
  • Validation: what the test suite checks against the papers.
  • Time series and lifetimes: chaotic transients, lifetime maps and exponential lifetime distributions (NJP 2004).
  • Bifurcations and edge state: continuation of equilibria and periodic orbits with BifurcationKit, compared with SIADS 2005; attractors of the minimal flow unit and their Lyapunov exponents; edge tracking.
  • Lagrangian descriptors: descriptor maps on slices of the nine-dimensional phase space, compared with the laminar basin boundary.

References

  • J. Moehlis, H. Faisst, B. Eckhardt, A low-dimensional model for turbulent shear flows, New J. Phys. 6, 56 (2004). (NJP)
  • J. Moehlis, H. Faisst, B. Eckhardt, Periodic orbits and chaotic sets in a low-dimensional model for shear flows, SIAM J. Appl. Dyn. Syst. 4, 352 (2005). (SIADS)
  • J. D. Skufca, J. A. Yorke, B. Eckhardt, Edge of chaos in a parallel shear flow, Phys. Rev. Lett. 96, 174101 (2006). Note: their nine-variable model is a related but different reduction (from A. Schmiegel's thesis), so we use their method, not their numbers.
  • A. M. Mancho, S. Wiggins, J. Curbelo, C. Mendoza, Lagrangian descriptors: A method for revealing phase space structures of general time dependent dynamical systems, Commun. Nonlinear Sci. Numer. Simul. 18, 3530 (2013).
