Claims we could not confirm or that differ from a source

  • The range "chaotic attractor for $335 \lesssim Re \lesssim 515$" does not appear in the papers. SIADS reports, for the minimal flow unit, a stable periodic orbit between torus bifurcations at $Re = 354.57$ and $507.40$, quasi-periodic and chaotic attractors below 354.57, and a boundary crisis at $Re \approx 335$. Following the attractor, we find a non-laminar attractor for roughly $332 \lesssim Re \lesssim 518$, chaotic only below $Re \approx 352$.
  • The papers do not state their criterion for laminarisation in the lifetime computations; we use fluctuation energy below $10^{-2}$.
  • NJP §5 sets $a_1 = 0$ in the initial conditions of the lifetime maps (not $a_1 = 1$), so $\sum_i a_i^2 = E - 1$; the nonzero lifetimes at $E - 1 \to 0$ in NJP Figs 6 and 9 are consistent with this reading, and we follow it.
